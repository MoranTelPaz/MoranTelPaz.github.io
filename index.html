CS & Computational Biology PhD Student · Hebrew University of Jerusalem

I am a PhD student in the Tommy Kaplan Lab at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem, working at the intersection of computer science and computational biology.

My research focuses on cell-free DNA (cfDNA), with emphasis on methylation and fragmentomics as non-invasive windows into the genome.

Prior to my PhD, I completed my MSc in the Noam Kaplan Lab at the Technion – Israel Institute of Technology.
